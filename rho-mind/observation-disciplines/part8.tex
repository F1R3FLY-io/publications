\section{What is shown}
\label{sec:shown}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{Two of the three components of a logic over a term language are
determined by its presentation.} The structural connectives are the constructors
lifted; the behavioural modalities are the context-labelled transitions lifted.
Neither involves a design decision once the presentation is fixed
(\S\ref{sec:three}). This is the content of the claim that the logics here are not
\emph{ad hoc}, and it is the one claim in the document that is fully established
rather than assembled.

\item \textbf{The collection container carries the substructural hierarchy.}
Commutativity of the container's aggregation is exchange; idempotence is
contraction; the unit laws are weakening (Principle~\ref{prin:dial}). A finer axis
--- which lattice operations the container makes primitive --- separates
intuitionistic, coalitional and linear settings that the coarse axis cannot
(Principle~\ref{prin:primitive}).

\item \textbf{Classical linear logic is a setting of that dial.} Phase semantics
is a two-layer container: an inner commutative monoid supplying the multiplicative,
an outer lattice supplying the additive, and a pole supplying involutive negation
(Observation~\ref{obs:twoconj}). Spatial logic's separating conjunction and linear
logic's $\otimes$ are the same operation (Observation~\ref{obs:sepisotimes}).

\item \textbf{Coalition logic's distinctive structure is predicted from container
closure alone.} A meet-free container forces a relational residue in place of
conjunction, and that residue is the compatibility relation of Gabbay's semiframes
(Observation~\ref{obs:semiframe}), arrived at independently.

\item \textbf{The observation reading of a generated logic requires no proof
theory} (Proposition~\ref{prop:sidestep}), which is why the range of logics
practically available for observation exceeds the range available for synthesis.

\item \textbf{Weight maps are graded clauses in disjunctive normal form}
(Theorem~\ref{thm:dnf}), so the partition discipline they required is unnecessary
and state-dependence is free (Proposition~\ref{prop:plasticity}).

\item \textbf{Which-path erasure is decided by the continuation, not the weights}
(Theorem~\ref{thm:scattering}, Corollary~\ref{cor:whichpath}), and needs no new
term former.

\item \textbf{Weighing is not selecting}, the two are the quantifier and selection
monads, and attainment of a section is a condition on the value algebra
(Proposition~\ref{prop:attain}) --- which independently picks out the algebra the
coalition row needs (Observation~\ref{obs:threeconstraints}).

\item \textbf{Choice strength can be written as clause shape}, one schema per
Weihrauch degree in a single syntactic slot (Table~\ref{tab:degrees}); the
extractable fragment and the choice-free fragment coincide
(Corollary~\ref{cor:extractable}); and choice type is not preserved by reduction
unless the \texttt{where} is stratified (Propositions~\ref{prop:srfail},
\ref{prop:srfix}).

\item \textbf{The measured results of Table~\ref{tab:collected}}, each produced by
a simulation shipped with its note.
\end{enumerate}

\section{What is assembled rather than established}
\label{sec:assembled}

Several of the more striking statements in this document are syntheses across
notes rather than theorems, and it should be possible for a reader to tell which.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item \textbf{The Girard-quantale criterion for negation in the graded sort}
(Observation~\ref{obs:negcriterion}). A short step from facts available since
\cite{Yetter90}, but the identification of the graded design's two-sortedness with
the failure of $\V$ to be a Girard quantale is made here for the first time and has
not been checked against every value algebra in use.
\item \textbf{The five-witness account of negation} (\S\ref{sec:negation}). The
five items are individually established in their sources; the claim that they are
one phenomenon is an organising thesis.
\item \textbf{The fibration reading of the hypothesis space}
(Observation~\ref{obs:twodof}). The base and the fibre are each established; that
their combination is the right way to describe a graded learner is a proposal, and
the coherence conditions on the fibration have not been checked.
\item \textbf{The identification of persistence with the exponential}
(Remark~\ref{rem:bangpersist}). Deliberately left as a conjecture.
\item \textbf{The modularity argument} (Observation~\ref{obs:nomerge}). That
merging two containers is the operation the no-go theorems obstruct is right; that
the obstruction holds for the particular pair at issue has not been checked, and
the theorems are not uniform.
\end{itemize}

\section{What is not shown}
\label{sec:notshown}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item \textbf{Adequacy is not general.} A generated logic separates exactly what
interaction separates only when the target specification supplies enough
structure. Many useful settings do not (\S\ref{sec:adequacy}).
\item \textbf{Decidability is not general.} Model-checking the ideal logic does not
terminate; the practical discipline is to restrict the fragment or the connectives,
at the cost of adequacy, and to bound the rest with a budget.
\item \textbf{The biology is not established.} The swarm results are untested
predictions; the framework does not out-predict evidence-accumulator models on
trajectories and is not a competing dynamical model.
\item \textbf{The ecological results are model results.} They are exact over their
stated worlds, and their pricing schedule is a choice that was varied. Band shapes
are robust to it; boundaries are not.
\item \textbf{The quantum reading is confined.} Unmodified \rhoc{} interferes only
on indistinguishable racers and only constructively; the graded language reaches
$\mathsf{PP}$ unless clauses are constrained to be isometric; and no claim of
quantum advantage is made or tested.
\item \textbf{Binding is scoped out} of the constructor-extension construction
that reconciles spatial strictness with adequacy, which remains its largest gap.
\end{itemize}

\section{Negative results, kept}
\label{sec:negatives}

Deliberately retained, because a summary that discards them misrepresents the
programme.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item \textbf{Grading can buy nothing.} At coalition size $k=13$ the tropical
$\min$ is zeroed by a single dissenter, so crisp and graded rungs return identical
rows. Grading pays only if the size threshold is relaxed too: two dials, not one.
\item \textbf{Cross-inhibition has an unexpected cost.} It suppresses correct
advocacy along with incorrect, so the swarm picks the better site slightly less
often with it than without ($.7235$ against $.7845$). Not predicted; open.
\item \textbf{The correct theory is a bad buy.} $\chi$ is dominated on the
noughts ladder, optimal in two cells of the phase grid, and its invasion threshold
is an endowment above $12{,}474$ price units.
\item \textbf{Depth can make a learner worse.} Deepening the theory while holding
the opening fixed moves win rate from $.833$ to $.548$.
\item \textbf{Confidence does not raise the mean.} It reduces variance. The crisp
forager has the highest terminal wealth conditional on survival, and the lowest
survival.
\end{itemize}

\section{Open problems}
\label{sec:open}

\begin{enumerate}[label=O\arabic*.,leftmargin=2.6em]
\item \textbf{The two gradings.} Prove or refute Conjecture~\ref{conj:twogradings}.
The obligation is to make ``the candidate container'' precise for clauses that use
the graded modality, where candidates at one site depend on transitions available
at another.
\item \textbf{The exponential.} Settle Remark~\ref{rem:bangpersist}: is persistence
the calculus's native $!$? The statement to prove concerns contraction on received
data in the presence of reflection.
\item \textbf{Isometry as a static judgement.} Decide isometry for clauses using
the graded modality, or show it undecidable. Until this exists, a graded program in
coherent mode should be read as post-selected.
\item \textbf{The converse of the Diaconescu cost.} We have that a choice-realising
resolver exhibits no interference deficit. The converse would need a topos of
graded predicates.
\item \textbf{Length-asymmetric reconvergence.} Does reflection supply a second,
non-local source of interference, by making the residual step count depend on which
racer won?
\item \textbf{Testimony with two coordinates.} Carry strength and confidence as a
pair with a lax algebra and a canonical aggregation order, and settle the
plagiarism equilibrium as a condition on the grading vector
(Observation~\ref{obs:minting}).
\item \textbf{Coherence of the fibration.} State and check the coherence conditions
for the hypothesis fibration of \S\ref{sec:fibration}, and give a search procedure
that moves in both coordinates with a principled exchange rate between a fibre step
and a base step.
\item \textbf{Which open the bees act on.} The discriminating experiment is
whether switchers --- scouts in two advocacy sets --- are over-represented among
bees that neither pipe nor stop-signal. It tests the logic rather than the
arithmetic.
\item \textbf{Learning the semitopology.} An observer must discover the set of
actionable coalitions rather than being given it. Identifying a topen is quadratic,
and a topen-boundary error is a shape error, unreachable by refinement.
\item \textbf{Heterogeneous quorums} and the compatibility relation as a cheaper
observation than the modality itself.
\item \textbf{The non-commutative row.} What does the generator emit when the
container is $\List$ --- for a term language with genuine sequence structure --- and
does it recover the Lambek calculus in the same sense that \S\ref{sec:linear}
recovers linear logic?
\item \textbf{Binding.} Extend the constructor-extension construction to binders,
which is the acknowledged gap in the reconciliation of spatial strictness with
adequacy.
\item \textbf{Modularity as a no-go theorem.} Settle
Observation~\ref{obs:nomerge} for the specific containers at issue --- is there a
distributive law between the bag monad and the opens of a semitopology? A negative
answer would turn the modularity of an aggregate observer from a prediction into a
theorem.
\item \textbf{Exchange between disciplines.} An aggregate mind composing several
disciplines must translate between them across the shared namespace. What is the
type of that translation, what does it cost, and what is lost --- given that the
containers, by hypothesis, do not merge?
\end{enumerate}

\section{Coda}
\label{sec:coda}

The thesis of this document can be put in one sentence, and it is worth putting
badly first so that the good version is legible.

The bad version: \emph{logic is relative to the machine}. That sounds either
trivial or nihilistic, and it is neither.

The good version: \emph{a logic is an observation discipline, generated from a term
language by four parameters, and a mortal thing does not choose its discipline
freely --- it can afford some settings and not others, and the settings it can
afford determine what distinctions exist for it.} The four parameters are the
signature, the rewrite rules, the container in which witnesses are collected, and
the algebra in which observations take their values. The first two are given by
what the thing is made of. The third says what kind of world it takes itself to be
in --- a world of sets, of resources, or of coalitions that can act. The fourth
says what an observation returns, and it is the one this document argues is
undersupplied in practice: almost everything is built with $\V = \B$, and a
learner built that way has, as we have seen measured, no coordinate in which to
record disappointment.

The instrument is small. It is a formula in a binder, with a value. What it does
is put the resolution of a system's own non-determinism inside the language, where
it can be reasoned about, priced, revised, and --- the point of the whole exercise
--- \emph{learned}.

And one consequence is worth leaving the reader with, because it is where the
account stops being about programming languages. If the containers do not merge,
then no observer of any size has a single logic. An aggregate mind is an assembly
of observation disciplines: one that counts, one that reads a room, one that reads
space, each generated from the same term structure by a different setting of the
dials, each separately priced, each separately acquirable --- and therefore each
separately absent. That is a claim about architecture, and it predicts exactly the
differentiated and dissociable capabilities that minds are in fact observed to
have. A mind is not one instrument. It is a workshop, and the interesting question
about any given one is which instruments are on the bench.
