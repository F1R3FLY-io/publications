The Prestige --- in the tradition of the magic trick whose structure
organizes this book --- is where the impossible is brought back.
The coin vanishes in the Pledge; it does something extraordinary in
the Turn; in the Prestige it reappears, and we are invited to
understand both what happened and why we could not see it
happening.

Chapter~\ref{ch:kant} has done the positioning.
It says what kind of thing the construction of the Turn is --- a
constructive metaphysics rather than a physics with philosophical
ambitions --- and whose question it is answering.
Three things remain.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{The View from Inside.} What an agent can and cannot
        know about its own substrate, at its own position, on its
        own budget --- the first-person / third-person interface as
        a theorem rather than a mystery.
        The sketch is in \emph{A View from Inside the Framework} at
        the close of the Turn, and the economics of it are in
        Section~\ref{sec:kant-affordable}; what is missing is the
        theorem.
  \item \textbf{The Blind Spot, Resolved.} Returning to the blind
        spot named in Chapter~\ref{chap:pledge-ch2}: why the
        computational substrate of mind is invisible to the
        processes running on it, and why the invisibility is
        structural rather than accidental.
  \item \textbf{Finding Mind.} The synthesis --- what it means to
        find mind in the structure of computation, and what that
        finding demands of us, scientifically, philosophically, and
        ethically.
\end{enumerate}
