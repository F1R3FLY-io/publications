  NEGATIVE RESULT, recorded rather than smoothed over.  The optimum
  is interior in both a and N even at c = 0, so token accounting
  already supplies an optimum that a rate-only account does not.
  But the optimal ladder here is LONG (N of 5 to 10), and real
  proofreading ladders are one or two steps.  The holding charge c
  tested here does not close that gap: raising c raises the cost
  without shortening the ladder, because higher yield at larger a
  dominates.  Whatever makes biological ladders short is not this
  term, and the note should not pretend otherwise.

E5  The ratchet and the register: energy bought instead of memory.
========================================================================
  A proofreading ladder is a sequential likelihood-ratio test whose
  accumulator has been replaced by position on the ladder.  It
  carries no state and cannot recover from one unlucky sample.
  The sequential test carries an accumulator, recovers, and wastes
  nothing -- but the accumulator must be HELD, and holding is
  priced.  m below is the price per step of holding it.
